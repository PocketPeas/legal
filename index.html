Privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026 · Applies to the Pocket Peas™ app for Android

In short
  • Everything you record about your child stays on your phone.
  • We do not run servers that receive your data, and we do not have an account for you.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell or share data.
  • You can erase everything at any time inside the app.

1. Who we are

Pocket Peas is made by Neha, Gurugram, Haryana, India ("we", "us"). You can reach us at teampocketpeas@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores

You choose what to enter. The app stores it in its own storage on your phone:

3. When data leaves your phone

Only in these cases:

  • When you share or export. If you share a report, save a backup file or save a photo, it goes where you send it. Whoever receives it handles it under their own rules.
  • Android backup. If Google backup is on for your phone, Android may include the app's data in your Google account backup. Google handles this under its own privacy policy. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.
  • Purchases. Payments for Pro go through Google Play. We receive only whether a purchase is active. We never see your card or bank details.

We do not use advertising, analytics, crash reporting or other third-party tracking tools.

4. Phone permissions

  • Notifications for reminders you turn on (meals, vaccines, medicines).
  • Run at start-up so reminders come back after the phone restarts.
  • Photos only when you choose to add a photo; the app opens your phone's own picker.
  • Internet for Google Play purchases and app updates.

5. Children

Pocket Peas is for parents and caregivers aged 18 or over. It is not meant to be used by children. Information about a child is entered by the parent or caregiver and stays on their phone. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we show no ads to anyone.

6. How long data is kept and how to delete it

  • Data stays on your phone until you delete it.
  • To erase everything: open the app, go to More › Settings and choose Erase all logs. You can also delete single entries anywhere in the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its data from the phone. Files you exported stay where you saved them.

7. Your rights

Because your data is on your phone, you can see, correct, export and delete it yourself at any time. Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or US state privacy laws), you may have further rights. Write to us with any request or question and we will reply within 7 days.

8. Keeping data safe

The PIN lock keeps casual users out of the app on your phone. It does not replace your phone's own screen lock, so please keep that on. If you lose your phone or uninstall the app without a backup, we cannot recover your data, because we never had a copy.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will tell you in the app before it applies.
